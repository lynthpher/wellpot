wellpot

A free generator for self-watering planters. Every pot is two printed parts: an outer pot with a water well below, and an inner pot that sits on a ledge above it. Fill the well through the built-in spout and the soil drinks from beneath for days at a time.

Pick a plant to start from a good fit, adjust anything, and download. Print in PETG, no supports — the watertight structure is built into the model, and the one slicer setting that matters (infill direction 30°) is included in the 3MF.

Free to print and share. If a pot turns out well, tips keep this growing.
